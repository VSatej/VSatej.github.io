At **Botson Labs**, I was part of the founding software engineering team working on **Powerbot**, an industrial IoT platform designed to help manufacturing facilities monitor electrical parameters, understand equipment performance, and identify opportunities for energy optimisation.

Working on Powerbot introduced me to the intersection of software engineering and physical industrial systems. Unlike conventional web applications, the platform involved collecting data from connected equipment, processing continuous telemetry, and making that information accessible through monitoring dashboards.

I worked on the data communication and ingestion pipeline using **MQTT** and **Telegraf**, enabling industrial monitoring data to flow into our backend infrastructure. For data storage, we used **PostgreSQL and TimescaleDB** to manage time-series measurements and support historical analysis.

On the application side, I contributed to backend services and developed interactive monitoring dashboards using **amCharts**. These dashboards helped visualise electrical parameters, equipment performance, power factor, and transformer monitoring information.

Another important part of the platform was automated alerting. I worked on integrating **WhatsApp and email notifications** to communicate critical conditions, including power factor abnormalities and transformer monitoring events. This helped make equipment monitoring more actionable rather than relying entirely on manual dashboard checks.

We deployed application components and monitoring services on **AWS EC2**, providing cloud-hosted access to the monitoring platform.

Being part of the founding engineering team gave me valuable experience in building an IoT product from the software side, understanding telemetry pipelines, working with time-series databases, and connecting industrial devices with cloud-based applications.

This experience sparked my interest in connected systems, industrial data engineering, and the role of software in improving real-world industrial operations.

**Technologies:** Python, MQTT, Telegraf, PostgreSQL, TimescaleDB, amCharts, AWS EC2, WhatsApp APIs, Email Notifications.
