After working as a Senior Software Engineer at **Innoctive**, I progressed to **Technical Team Lead**, taking on broader responsibilities for the development and delivery of **CargoFL**.

Working on the **Alkem** engagement, I continued contributing to the evolution of the logistics platform while taking greater ownership of engineering activities across its core modules: Master Data, Execution and Billing.

My responsibilities expanded beyond individual feature development to include understanding business requirements, translating them into technical tasks, coordinating development activities, and ensuring consistent implementation across interconnected modules.

A major focus was coordinating the development of end-to-end transportation workflows. These covered Delivery Order (DO) processing, trip creation and validation, SAP shipment number generation, gate actions, and transportation status updates.

I was also involved in coordinating trip costing and billing workflows, where transportation execution data needed to flow consistently into operational and financial processes. These integrations required close attention to business rules, data validation, and communication between different application modules.

On the engineering side, I worked closely with the team on backend development using **Python and Flask**, frontend applications built with **Next JS**, and database management using **MySQL**. I also supported SAP REST API integrations, AWS infrastructure, and Docker-based deployment workflows.

As Technical Team Lead, I took responsibility for breaking down requirements, planning development activities, reviewing code, supporting technical decisions, and helping developers resolve complex implementation challenges.

Moving into technical leadership allowed me to combine hands-on engineering experience with team coordination and delivery ownership. It strengthened my ability to understand enterprise business processes, guide technical implementation, and deliver integrated software solutions in a production environment.

**Technologies:** Python, Flask, Next JS, MySQL, SAP REST APIs, JSON, AWS EC2, S3, RDS, Docker, CI/CD.
