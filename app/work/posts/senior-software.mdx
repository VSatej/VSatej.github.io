At **Innoctive**, I worked as a Senior Software Engineer on **CargoFL**, an enterprise supply chain and logistics platform designed to digitise transportation operations. One of my key engagements was working on the logistics operations of **Alkem**, where I contributed to developing workflows across different stages of the transportation lifecycle.

My role involved building backend services, developing application features, and integrating enterprise systems to connect operational and business processes. I worked across several core modules, including **Master Data, Execution, Billing, and Procurement**.

A significant part of my work focused on the Execution module, where I developed workflows covering Delivery Order (DO) processing, validation, and trip creation. This included handling invalid trip scenarios, managing transportation information, and integrating with SAP to generate and synchronise shipment numbers.

I also worked on transportation milestone management through gate actions, along with the generation of **Lorry Receipts (LR)** documents. These workflows connected shipment execution with the documentation and operational requirements involved in completing a trip.

Another important area was trip costing and its connection with billing processes. I worked on application logic supporting transportation expense calculations and the flow of operational information between these modules, helping maintain consistency across logistics and financial workflows.

From a technical perspective, I primarily worked with **Python and Flask** to develop backend APIs and business logic. I also contributed to frontend development using **Next JS**, managed relational data using **MySQL**, and integrated external enterprise services through SAP REST APIs and JSON-based communication.

Working on CargoFL gave me practical experience in understanding complex enterprise logistics processes and translating business requirements into functional software. It also strengthened my understanding of API integrations, database-driven applications, and building systems that support real-world business operations.

**Technologies:** Python, Flask, JavaScript, Next.js, React, MySQL, SAP REST APIs, JSON, AWS EC2, S3, RDS, Docker, CI/CD.
